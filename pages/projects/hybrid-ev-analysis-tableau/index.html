Hybrid and Electric Vehicle Adoption Visualization

This project creates a data story in Tableau exploring hybrid and electric vehicle adoption in the U.S.

Description

Hybrid and Electric Vehicles are a growing sector of the automobile market in the U.S. While gas-powered vehicles continue to make up the majority of the market, these gas alternatives are making up a larger percentage of the market each year due to their superior fuel efficiency and environmental friendliness, and due to increasing infrastructure support and manufacturer adoption. It is not unreasonable to assume that in the near future, gas alternative cars will comprise an even larger portion of the market. This report seeks to assess the current state of Hybrid and Electric Vehicle (H/EV) adoption in the United States in 2026 by visualizing data concerning H/EV sales in Tableau, combining them into several dashboards comprising a single story, and publishing that story to the Tableau Public web platform.
